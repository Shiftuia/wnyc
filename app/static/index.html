An apartment listing shows the price. It does not show whether a wheelchair user can get out of the neighbourhood. This map does.

Open the map Watch it answer a question

    1. Ask in your own words

      “I use a wheelchair, $3,500, I work at 350 5th Ave.” An AI model analyses the city for you and explains its top three.

    2. Play with the map

      Drag the budget, tilt into 3D, switch layers: broken elevators, bus stops, hills, housing lotteries.

    3. Check a real trip

      A to B by accessible subway, accessible Uber/Lyft, or wheels — time, cost, and whether you can do it alone.
