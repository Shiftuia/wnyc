        Proxies, not guarantees
        • Rent is Zillow’s typical market rent for the area (Oct 2026), not the rent of an accessible apartment.
        • Housing figures are proxies: post-1991 buildings fall under Fair Housing Act design rules; elevator buildings can be step-free. Neither confirms an accessible unit is available.
        • One-seat ride means an accessible station near home and one near work share a line.
        • Trip check uses the subway timetable through accessible stations only, and real Uber/Lyft trips for the chosen time of day. Wheeling speed, a 1.3 detour factor and 3 minutes per elevator are assumptions. Buses, platform gaps and slopes are not modelled.
        • Ride-hail numbers come from completed trips only (Sept 2026). Requests that never found a car are not in the data.
        • Elevator outages are a snapshot of the MTA feed taken . Forecast dates for new accessible stations are the MTA’s and have slipped before.
        • Hilliness is the elevation range across a few homes per zone — a rough proxy, not street slope.
